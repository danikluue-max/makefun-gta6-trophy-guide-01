The best GTA VI trophy guide: all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies (1 platinum,
2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze) with their official names and descriptions,
a guide in our own words for every single one — each citing its sources,
with inline citation numbers on every step — a checklist that saves in
your browser, 24 interactive progress counters (meditation spots, stunts,
bank vaults, …) that persist and show on the checklist rows, a three-stage
road to the platinum with per-stage counts, a next-up banner that points
at the most urgent trophy first, a missable-trophy watch list (one
possibly missable trophy, unconfirmed — the guides disagree and this
guide says so), a spoiler mode that hides story trophies until earned,
search and filters by kind, grade and roadmap stage, and progress stats
with an hours-left estimate. Six sources (PowerPyx, KeenGamer, GTABase,
OutputLag, GTA Zone, Push Square via Exophase), all checked 19 Nov 2026.
Fully offline static files: no network, no account, no ads.
